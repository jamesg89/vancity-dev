What changes the price

Two businesses with the same revenue can be very different to look after. These are the five things we look at when we scope a fee — and the only reasons it would ever change later.

  • Transaction volumeThe single biggest driver. 80 transactions a month is not 800, however similar the revenue looks.
  • Number of accounts and entitiesEvery bank, card, loan and merchant account is another reconciliation; every entity is another set of books and another return.
  • ComplexityInventory, job costing, WIP, multi-state payroll or sales tax, foreign accounts, investor reporting.
  • Condition of the recordsClean books cost less to maintain. Catch-up is quoted separately, once, so it never becomes an open-ended bill.
  • Headcount and payroll frequencyWeekly payroll for 22 people is a different job from monthly payroll for 3.

Pricing questions

Why don’t you just publish one price?

Because it would be wrong for most people. The starting prices above are real — they’re what a business with clean records, one bank account and modest volume actually pays. Your proposal states one number for your situation, and we’d rather spend twenty minutes getting it right than quote something we have to revise.

Will I get a bill after every phone call?

No. That’s the main reason people leave their old accountant, and we don’t bill by the hour at all. Calls, emails and quick questions are included in your monthly fee.

What if my business changes mid-year?

We re-scope and tell you the new fee before the work happens. Taking on payroll, adding an entity or moving into a second state all change the price; growing revenue on its own usually doesn’t.

Do you require an annual contract?

No. Monthly packages are month to month. The annual option is simply a discount for paying up front — two months free — and it’s entirely optional.

How much is catch-up work?

It’s quoted per month of back work after a short records review, usually in a single fixed fee with a payment plan if the project is large. You’ll know the total before we start, and it’s separate from your ongoing monthly fee.

Can I pay by card?

Yes — card or ACH, monthly, through our payment portal. Invoices are issued on the first of the month.
