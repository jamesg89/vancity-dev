Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with businesses outside {{CITY}}?

Yes. About half our clients are in {{SERVICE_AREA}} and come to the office; the rest work with us entirely online, anywhere in {{STATE}} and beyond. Same advisor, same response promise, video instead of a conference room.

How is pricing set?

We quote a fixed monthly or fixed per-return fee based on scope — transaction volume, number of accounts, payroll headcount, entity type and how far behind the records are. You get it in writing before any work starts, and it doesn’t change unless the scope does. We don’t bill by the hour, so a phone call never costs you anything.

Can you catch me up on back taxes or back books?

That’s a large part of what we do. Catch-up work is quoted separately after a short records review, then you move onto a normal monthly fee. Unfiled returns are usually best handled before the IRS contacts you — the sooner the better for penalty relief.

What’s the difference between a CPA and a bookkeeper?

A bookkeeper records what happened: categorising transactions, reconciling accounts, closing the month. A CPA is state-licensed, bound by continuing education and ethics rules, and can sign returns, represent you before the IRS and advise on structure and strategy. Most small businesses need both — which is why we do both under one fee.

How do I send documents securely?

Through the client portal: encrypted upload, multi-factor login, and e-signature for returns. Please don’t email tax documents, SSNs or account numbers, and don’t put them in the form on this page — email isn’t a secure channel.

Do you handle IRS letters?

Yes. Send us the notice through the portal and we’ll tell you what it is, whether it’s correct and what it will take to resolve — usually within {{RESPONSE_PROMISE}}. {{OWNER_NAME}} can represent you directly before the IRS. Most notices are routine; a few are not, and the difference matters.
